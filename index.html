Bulletin No. 006  ·  Miami International Autodrome  ·  2026

The Stewards of the AMF1 Intern Prediction Championship, having reviewed the timesheets, lap time submissions, and frankly some very questionable Q4 constructor picks, hereby issue the following official findings regarding Round 6, the Miami Grand Prix.

Stewards' Commendations
Ellie Kendall 21 pts · Round Winner

Ellie Kendall is hereby found to have been absolutely on the money in Miami, storming to the round win with 21 points and a 7-point gap to second. The Stewards note that while the actual F1 drivers were sweating in the Florida heat, Ellie was coolly predicting podiums from the comfort of the office. The championship has taken notice. Dangerous.

Louis Tutin 20 pts · P2 This Round

A strong 20 points for Louis, who quietly goes about his business like a backmarker team that somehow keeps finishing in the points. From zero in Melbourne to podium pace in Miami — the Stewards acknowledge this is very much not dead rubber form.

Shaheer Aamer 19 pts · P3 This Round

Shaheer takes P3 on the round and quietly moves up the overall standings. The Stewards are watching. The team radio from his race engineer (probably) was: "Good job, stay out of trouble, the championship is long." Sage advice.

Stewards' Investigations
Gian Marco Tornaghi 3 pts · Last of Submitters

The Stewards regret to inform Gian Marco that 3 points is, statistically speaking, not ideal. The panel notes that even with the charitable addition of correct constructor picks, the overall return from Miami was — and the Stewards are choosing their words carefully here — catastrophic. An investigation into strategy is recommended. The Stewards wish him better luck in Canada, though they're not optimistic.

Samantha Tullie 4 pts · A Difficult Weekend

After sharing the championship lead not long ago, Samantha drops with only 4 points in Miami. The Stewards recognise that in Formula 1, momentum can shift in an instant — and this was very much an instant. The panel hope the flight to Canada includes some time for reflection.

⚑ Next Race: Canadian Grand Prix — Circuit Gilles Villeneuve

The championship now heads north to Montreal for Round 7, the Canadian Grand Prix. The Circuit Gilles Villeneuve is famously unforgiving — much like the Stewards — with its wall of champions ready to claim another victim. Will Ollie extend his lead? Will anyone dethrone him? Will Gian Marco score more than 3 points? The Stewards believe only one of these is genuinely in doubt.

The form for Round 7 is now live. The Stewards remind all participants that the window closes at 17:30 on Friday. Late submissions will be treated with the same sympathy the Stewards extend to unsafe releases: none whatsoever.
