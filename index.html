Bulletin No. 007  ·  Circuit Gilles Villeneuve, Montréal  ·  2025

The Stewards of the AMF1 Intern Prediction Championship, convening in an unusually orderly session uninterrupted by wildlife of any kind, hereby issue the following official findings regarding Round 7, the Canadian Grand Prix. The paddock has noted the absence of groundhogs, geese, and other creatures previously known to delay proceedings. The Stewards are frankly suspicious.

Stewards' Commendations — Round 7
Shaheer Aamer 18 pts · Joint Round Winner · ↑4 to P3 Overall

Shaheer rockets from P7 to P3 overall on the back of an 18-point Montreal haul. Four positions gained in a single round is the kind of form that makes championship leaders nervous. The Stewards formally place Ollie Oldrey on notice. Shaheer is coming, and unlike the geese, he gave absolutely no warning.

Louis Tutin 18 pts · Joint Round Winner · ↑4 to P5 Overall

Louis also bags 18 points and climbs four places to P5. At this rate of ascent, the Stewards calculate Louis will overtake Ollie sometime around the Belgian Grand Prix — assuming Ollie doesn't score another 18 points first, which he probably will. Still: genuinely dangerous form.

Vedant Tayade 17 pts · ↑7 — Biggest Climber of the Round

The Stewards award the unofficial Driver of the Day to Vedant, who vaults an extraordinary seven places from P15 to P8. Seven. In one round. From the back of the midfield to the edge of the top ten in a single afternoon. The Stewards are formally requesting Vedant's strategy notes. He had previously scored zero in Suzuka, which makes this even more remarkable — or suggests he simply guessed better this time. Either way: hats off.

James Mitchell 18 pts · ↑2 to P14 Overall

James scores a joint-best 18 points in Montreal, climbing two places and quietly making the case that his earlier low scores were just a warm-up. The Stewards note that P14 is still P14, but the trajectory is pointing firmly in the right direction. More of this please, James.

Stewards' Investigations
Anthony Zehnder 0 pts · ↓7 — Biggest Faller of the Round

Anthony entered Montreal in P3 overall. He exits in P10. A seven-place drop in a single round is the sort of thing that happens when you don't submit a form — and indeed, Anthony did not submit a form. The Stewards note that Anthony had 52 points before Montreal and still has 52 points after Montreal, which is a mathematically accurate way of saying this was a catastrophic weekend. The panel wishes him a swift recovery and strongly recommends remembering to fill in the form for Silverstone.

Ellie Kendall 9 pts · ↓3 to P7 Overall

After winning the Miami round outright, Ellie drops three places in Montreal with 9 points. The Stewards acknowledge that following up a dominant performance is never easy — just ask any constructor who led the championship in April. The panel fully expects a bounce-back, but are keeping a watchful eye.
